Hi! I’m Paridhi, a creative technologist in New York with a BFA in Design and Technology from Parsons School of Design.

I design and develop interactive interfaces and experiences that encourage observation, connection, and curiosity. My focus is on creating encounters with technology—moments where interfaces feel less like utilities and more like spaces for reflection, play, and learning. These works take various forms, from AI-based web interfaces and narrative 3D worlds to spatial installations and sensor-based physical experiences.

I’ve previously collaborated with IBM Quantum and the DLR Institute for AI Safety and Security to create interactive exhibits that translated complex systems into human-scale interactions.
